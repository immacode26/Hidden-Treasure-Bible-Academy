Prayer Changes Everything

Prayer is one of God's greatest gifts to His children. Through prayer we worship God, confess our sins, give thanks, seek guidance, intercede for others, and grow in our relationship with Him.

Why Prayer Matters

Prayer is not merely speaking words; it is communicating with our Heavenly Father. Jesus often withdrew to lonely places to pray, teaching us that a strong prayer life is essential for every believer.

The Bible encourages us to pray continually, trusting that God hears the prayers of His people and answers according to His perfect wisdom and love.

🙏 Adoration

Begin prayer by praising God for His holiness, greatness, mercy, faithfulness, love and power.

❤️ Confession

Confess your sins honestly before God and receive His forgiveness through Jesus Christ.

🙌 Thanksgiving

Thank God for salvation, family, health, His daily provision and every blessing He has given.

🤲 Supplication

Present your needs before God with faith, believing that He knows what is best for you.

The Lord's Prayer

"Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name, your kingdom come, your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Give us today our daily bread. And forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one."

Matthew 6:9-13
